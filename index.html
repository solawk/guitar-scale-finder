    Help

    Scale finder

    Click any fret or open string to toggle a note. Multiple notes per string are allowed. Bright cells contain every selected pitch class; dimmed cells remain available for comparison. Click a scale twice to turn its overlay on and off.

    Chords & sequences

    Choose one position per string. Click it again, or click its string label, to mute the string. Select a chord interpretation if more than one name fits. Custom note sets need a root. Save adds the chord to the temporary list and clears the fretboard.

    Chord order is matched against common patterns, including rotations and repeated cycles. Partial matches are labeled. Power chords lack a third, so their major/minor interpretation depends on context. A match is a possible reading, not a unique identification of a key. Rhythm is not analyzed.

    Progression builder

    Use the current chord or a saved chord. Pick a pattern, then click a compatible degree. Other chords are calculated from that anchor. Incompatible degrees are disabled. Colors show compact voicings or all chord tones. Shared positions have multiple color sectors. Toggle individual chords to compare them.

    Power-chord anchors use power chords for the other steps by default. Turn the option off to use the template’s full chords. Compact voicings contain every chord tone within a four-fret span; finger assignments are not evaluated. The entered anchor voicing stays unchanged.

    Keyboard & storage

    Use arrow keys within a fretboard or scale table, Home/End for the first/last fret or tonic, and Enter/Space to select. Arrow keys also switch tool tabs. The saved sequence stays in this browser tab across reloads, until the tab closes. Chord and note data is not uploaded.

    Standard tuning: E₂ A₂ D₃ G₃ B₃ E₄, thin string at the top. Melodic minor is the ascending/jazz form. Note labels use chromatic sharp/flat names rather than theoretical enharmonic spelling.
